E1
EPISODE 01 · PS-106

THE FORENSIC ANALYST

AI-powered email threat detection, geolocation and forensic intelligence platform. Flags malicious emails, verifies sender authenticity and generates evidence reports.

PythonFlaskscikit-learnLeaflet.jsReportLabdnspython
Behind the scenes
  • Seven core modules, including a Naïve Bayes phishing classifier and GeoIP clustering on a Leaflet threat map.
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC sender verification, with automated PDF forensic reports.
  • Gmail and Outlook IMAP for live ingestion, SHA-256 chain-of-custody, and a feedback loop that retrains from analyst corrections.
